💧 Lu Aqua — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Lu Aqua ("we", "the app") handles your data. Lu Aqua is published by Girgis Bayoumi (gogy.bairy@gmail.com). By installing and using Lu Aqua you agree to this policy.

The short version:
  • Lu Aqua stores all your data locally on your device.
  • We do not run a server. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data.
  • Optional Health Connect sync is off by default. When you enable it, Lu Aqua reads your steps from Health Connect and writes your daily hydration there — both stay on your device.
  • The "Smart Coach" hints on the dashboard are computed entirely on-device. No data is sent to any AI service.
  • Two Google services are used in-app: AdMob (ads) and Play Billing (subscriptions). Their own privacy policies cover the data they handle.

1. What data Lu Aqua stores on your device

The following is saved inside the app's private storage (Android's local SQLite database + SharedPreferences). It never leaves your device unless you choose to share it manually.

CategoryExamples
ProfileDisplay name, weight, activity level, wake/sleep times, profile photo (optional)
Hydration logEach drink you log: type, amount, timestamp
RemindersTimes and labels of the water reminders you set
PreferencesTheme, language, dark mode, daily-goal mode, Ramadan mode
Mood entriesOptional emoji-based mood you tap for the day
CachingLast-known outdoor temperature for the Smart Goal feature

You can erase all of this at any time via Settings → Reset All Data, or by uninstalling the app.

2. Permissions and why

  • Notifications — to send drink reminders, daily tips, and alarm-action prompts.
  • Schedule exact alarms — so reminders fire at the precise minute you set.
  • Ignore battery optimizations — optional, helps reminders stay reliable on aggressive battery-saving phones.
  • Use full-screen intent — so the alarm screen can appear over the lock screen, like a regular alarm clock.
  • Internet — used only by Google AdMob to fetch ads and Google Play Billing to verify subscriptions.
  • Receive boot completed — so reminders are re-armed after a phone restart.
  • Foreground service / Vibrate / Wake lock — for alarm delivery and screen wake.
  • Health Connect (optional, off by default) — read steps and active calories so the daily goal can adjust to your activity, and write today's hydration so other health apps see it. Only active when you enable it in Settings → Health Connect sync.
  • Activity recognition (optional) — backs step reads on Android 10–13 devices that don't have Health Connect. Only requested when the Health Connect toggle is on.

The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, GPS location, SMS, or storage outside its own sandbox.

3. Approximate location via IP (no GPS)

For the Smart Goal feature Lu Aqua queries the public service ipwho.is to derive an approximate latitude/longitude from your device's IP address, and uses api.open-meteo.com to fetch the current outdoor temperature. The result is cached for six hours locally. We do not store or transmit your IP address, coordinates, or city to any server we control. GPS is not used and we never request location permission.

4. Health Connect integration (opt-in)

Lu Aqua optionally integrates with Android Health Connect — the system data hub that other health apps (Fitbit, Samsung Health, Google Fit successor, etc.) read and write from. This integration is off by default and only activates when you enable it in Settings → Health Connect sync.

When enabled, Lu Aqua:

  • Reads your daily step count and active calories so the in-app daily goal can rise on more active days.
  • Writes your daily hydration total (in litres) so apps that watch Health Connect can summarise your water intake alongside other vitals.

Health Connect data is stored locally on your device by the Health Connect system app. We do not read, copy, or transmit Health Connect data to any server. You can revoke Lu Aqua's access at any time from the Health Connect app or by turning off the toggle in Lu Aqua Settings.

On Android 10–13 devices without Health Connect, the same toggle uses the legacy Activity Recognition permission to read steps from the device's pedometer. Same scope, same local-only behaviour.

5. Smart Coach (on-device insights)

Lu Aqua generates personalised hydration tips from your own logged data using a set of on-device rules. This is not a cloud AI service — no data is sent off your device for analysis. The "Smart Coach" card on the dashboard reads from your local SQLite store and produces tips like "You're behind today's goal" or "You consistently miss morning hydration". If you disable the dashboard card it stays hidden, and no analysis runs.

6. Wear OS companion (future feature)

Lu Aqua publishes today's hydration totals over the Android Wearable Data Layer so that a future Wear OS watch app can mirror them on your wrist. No companion watch app ships with this release; the phone-side publisher runs silently in the background. If you don't have a paired Wear OS watch with a Lu Aqua app installed, nothing happens. No data leaves your phone–watch pairing.

7. Third-party services

Google AdMob (ads): Lu Aqua shows banner and rewarded ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and ad-interaction data per Google's policies. Subscribers on Lite tier and above do not see banner ads; Standard tier and above remove interstitials as well. See Google's ads privacy policy.

Google Play Billing (subscriptions): Payment is processed by Google Play. Lu Aqua receives only an entitlement signal (which tier you bought and when it expires). Card details and personal payment data are handled by Google. See Google's privacy policy.

8. Children

Lu Aqua is intended for general audiences (13+). The app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a minor has shared personal information through Lu Aqua, email gogy.bairy@gmail.com and we will help. Note: Lu Aqua stores no data on our servers — anything you want removed must be cleared from the device.

9. Your rights

  • Delete: Settings → Reset All Data, or uninstall.
  • Export: use the "Share Achievement" / CSV export features (Pro tier).
  • Cancel subscription or stop ads: Google Play → Subscriptions.

For any privacy question or request, email gogy.bairy@gmail.com.
