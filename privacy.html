💧 SmartSip - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 14, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to SmartSip! This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our hydration tracking app.

Developer: Girgis Adel
Contact: gogy.bairy@gmail.com
App: SmartSip - Smart Water Tracker

Information We Collect

SmartSip respects your privacy. Here's what we collect and why:

1. Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Name - to personalize your experience
  • Email address - for login and account recovery
  • Password - stored securely (hashed, never in plain text)
  • Profile picture - optional, you choose to upload it

2. Health & Hydration Data

To help you track your water intake, we collect:

  • Weight - to calculate your recommended daily water goal
  • Activity level - to adjust hydration recommendations
  • Sleep schedule - to set smart reminder times
  • Water consumption logs - the drinks you record daily
  • Daily streaks and progress - your achievements over time

This data is used only to provide the app's tracking features and is never shared with third parties.

3. App Settings (Stored Locally)

  • Theme color and dark mode preference
  • Language (English/Arabic)
  • Reminder schedule
  • Custom drink sizes

4. Advertising Data (Google AdMob)

SmartSip uses Google AdMob to display ads in the free version. AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID
  • IP address (for general location only)
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • App usage data for ad personalization

This data is governed by Google's privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can opt out of personalized ads via your device settings:
Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

5. App Updates

The app checks for updates by sending only the current app version. No personal data is transmitted during this check.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide hydration tracking and reminder services
  • Calculate personalized water intake goals
  • Sync your data across your devices
  • Send you reminders to drink water
  • Display progress charts and statistics
  • Award badges and achievements
  • Show health-related articles
  • Display ads (free version only)

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on:

  • Your device - app settings, offline cache
  • Our secure servers - account info, drink logs, sync data

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encrypted password storage (bcrypt hashing)
  • JWT tokens for authentication
  • HTTPS for all data transmission
  • Regular security updates
Note: While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no online service is 100% secure. Use a strong, unique password for your account.

Data Sharing

We do NOT:

  • Sell your personal data to anyone
  • Share your health data with third parties
  • Use your information for marketing emails (unless you opt in)
  • Track you across other apps

We DO use these third-party services:

  • Google AdMob - for displaying ads (free users only)
  • Email service (Gmail SMTP) - for sending verification codes and password resets

Children's Privacy

SmartSip is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at gogy.bairy@gmail.com and we will delete it immediately.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data anytime through the app
  • Export your data as PDF reports
  • Update your profile information at any time
  • Delete your account and all associated data by contacting us
  • Opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings
  • Withdraw consent by uninstalling the app

Premium Subscription

If you upgrade to SmartSip Premium:

  • Payments are processed by Google Play Billing
  • We do not store your payment information
  • Subscriptions can be managed in Google Play settings
  • Premium removes ads and unlocks additional features

Data Retention

We retain your account data as long as your account is active. If you delete your account:

  • All personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Anonymized usage statistics may be retained for analytics
  • Backups may contain your data for up to 90 days before being purged

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be notified through the app. Continued use of SmartSip after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Compliance

SmartSip complies with:

  • Google Play Developer Program Policies
  • Google Play Families Policy (where applicable)
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) principles
