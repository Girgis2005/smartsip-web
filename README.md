# SmartSip 💧

**Stay hydrated, stay healthy.**

SmartSip is a smart hydration tracking mobile app that helps you build healthy water-drinking habits.

## 🌟 Features

- 💧 **Smart Tracking** - Log every drink with one tap
- 🔔 **Smart Reminders** - Adaptive notifications based on your sleep schedule
- 📊 **Charts & Stats** - Weekly and monthly progress visualizations
- 🏆 **Badges & Achievements** - Stay motivated with rewards
- 🎯 **Challenges** - Compete with friends and join community challenges
- 📚 **Health Articles** - Daily tips about hydration and wellness
- 📤 **PDF Reports** - Export your data to share with your doctor
- 🌙 **Dark Mode** - Easy on your eyes
- 🎨 **24 Themes** - Customize your experience
- 🌍 **Bilingual** - English and Arabic
- 📱 **Offline Ready** - Works without internet, syncs later
